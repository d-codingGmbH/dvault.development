## Oracle Threshold Decision

- Decision: keep the Oracle direct optimized batching safety cap at 10000 satellite operations and keep stagedOracleBulk=not-selected-no-measured-win.
- Measured rationale: Oracle direct optimized rows at the current 10000-satellite boundary selected OracleDataVaultSaveStrategy and beat the provider-neutral DVault fallback, but they still trailed the conventional EF comparison rows.
- High-volume rationale: the 100000-satellite customer-profile-scale-10000x10 optimized-baseline row fell back with fallbackCauses=OracleMaximumSatelliteOperationThreshold and remained much slower than conventional EF, so the retained cap avoids promoting an unproven larger Oracle path.
- Diagnostics posture: OracleMinimumOperationThreshold remains visible for below-threshold batches, and OracleMaximumSatelliteOperationThreshold remains visible whenever the high-volume cap drives fallback.
